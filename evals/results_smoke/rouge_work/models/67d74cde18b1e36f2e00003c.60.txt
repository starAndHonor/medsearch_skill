RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR)-based predictor for peptide-MHC (pMHC) binding mode identification.
It is trained to predict the most accurate ranking of an ensemble of pMHC conformations.
The binding of peptides to class-I Major Histocompability Complex (MHC) receptors and their subsequent recognition downstream by T-cell receptors are crucial processes for most multicellular organisms to be able to fight various diseases.
RankMHC can be used with many pMHC structural modeling tools that use different structural modeling protocols.
