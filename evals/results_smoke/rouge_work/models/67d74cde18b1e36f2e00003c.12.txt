RankMHC is an LTR-based (learning to rank) model specifically designed to predict the most accurate binding mode for class-I peptide-MHC structural models.
It ranks an ensemble of pMHC conformations to select the best binding pose and has been trained specifically for this task.
The results show that it outperforms traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and earlier machine learning-based predictors.
Additionally, RankMHC is versatile and can be used with various pMHC structural modeling tools that utilize different protocols.
