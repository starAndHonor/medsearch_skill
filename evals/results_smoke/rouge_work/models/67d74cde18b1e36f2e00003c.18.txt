RankMHC is a computational tool used for predicting and ranking peptide-MHC (Major Histocompatibility Complex) binding affinities.
It is designed to help identify potential epitopes for vaccine development and immunotherapy by evaluating how well different peptides bind to MHC molecules.
RankMHC uses machine learning algorithms and large datasets of known peptide-MHC interactions to provide accurate predictions, aiding researchers in understanding immune responses and designing targeted treatments.
