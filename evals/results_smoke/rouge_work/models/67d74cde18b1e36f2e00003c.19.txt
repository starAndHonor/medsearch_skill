RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR) based predictor that aims to identify the most accurate binding mode or peptide pose from an ensemble of candidate peptide-MHC structural models.
It is specifically trained to predict the ranking of peptide-MHC conformations such that the top-ranked pose is the one closest to the native structure.
RankMHC outperforms classical protein-ligand scoring functions and previous machine learning-based binding pose predictors in identifying the binding mode of peptides to MHC class I receptors, which is crucial for developing therapies against diseases like cancer, viral and bacterial infections.
