RankMHC is a machine learning-based tool that aims to identify the correct binding mode (or pose) of a peptide to a Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) class I receptor from an ensemble of candidate peptide-MHC structural models.
It frames the problem as a learning-to-rank task and uses a ranking model trained on structural features to predict the ranking of the candidate poses, with the top-ranked pose being predicted as the most accurate representation of the actual binding mode.
RankMHC outperforms traditional scoring functions and previous machine learning methods for this task.
