RankMHC is a computational approach designed to evaluate and rank the structural models of Class I peptide MHC (Major Histocompatibility Complex) complexes.
This method employs machine learning techniques to assess how effectively these complexes can present antigens for recognition by T cells, which is crucial for immune response modulation.
By analyzing the 3D structures of peptide MHC interactions, RankMHC predicts their functional effectiveness, such as antigen presentation efficiency and stability.
This tool is particularly valuable in immunology research for applications like vaccine development and understanding immune system dynamics.
