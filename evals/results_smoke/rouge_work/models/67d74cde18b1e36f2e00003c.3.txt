RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR) predictor that identifies the most accurate peptide-MHC (pMHC) binding mode from an ensemble of conformations.
It outperforms traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous Machine Learning-based predictors by ranking poses based on their similarity to the native structure.
Compatible with multiple pMHC structural modeling tools and protocols, RankMHC is a versatile tool for peptide antigen identification, particularly useful in developing therapies against bacterial, viral infections, and cancer.
Its predictive capability enhances the accuracy of selecting the best peptide pose for immune response therapies.
