RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR) predictor for peptide-MHC binding mode identification.
It is specifically trained to predict the most accurate ranking of an ensemble of pMHC conformations.
RankMHC outperforms classical peptide-ligand scoring functions, as well as previous Machine Learning (ML)-based binding pose predictors.
It can be used with many pMHC structural modeling tools that use different structural modeling protocols.
