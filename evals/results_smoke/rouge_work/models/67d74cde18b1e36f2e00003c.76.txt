RankMHC is a machine learning-based predictor that employs a learning-to-rank (LTR) strategy to identify and select the most accurate structural models of peptide–MHC class I binding modes.
It does this by ranking an ensemble of pMHC conformations generated from various structural modeling tools, ensuring that the top-ranked model is the one most likely to reflect the correct binding mode.
By outperforming traditional peptide–ligand scoring functions and earlier machine learning approaches, RankMHC demonstrates improved accuracy in identifying optimal pMHC structures.
Its design allows it to be used alongside different pMHC modeling protocols, making it versatile and effective in the structural modeling of peptide–MHC interactions.
