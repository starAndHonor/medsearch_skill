RankMHC is an LTR-based predictor specifically designed to identify and rank peptide-MHC (pMHC) binding modes with high accuracy.
It focuses on predicting the most accurate ranking of pMHC conformations, aiding in understanding how peptides bind to MHC molecules.
This tool is crucial for immunology research, as it supports the study of T cell recognition and immune responses, potentially advancing disease treatment by targeting pMHC complexes.
RankMHC's effectiveness is highlighted by its prediction success rates, making it a valuable asset for future research and design in TCR/pMHC complex interactions.
