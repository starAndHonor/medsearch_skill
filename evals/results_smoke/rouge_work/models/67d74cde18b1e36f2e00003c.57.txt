RankMHC is a machine learning-based predictor designed to identify and rank peptide-MHC (pMHC) binding conformations with high accuracy.
It utilizes a learning-to-rank (LTR) approach and has been shown to outperform traditional peptide-ligand scoring methods and previous ML-based predictors.
Additionally, RankMHC is compatible with various pMHC structural modeling tools and protocols.
