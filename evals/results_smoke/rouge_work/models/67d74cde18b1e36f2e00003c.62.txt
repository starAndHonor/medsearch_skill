RankMHC is an LTR-based (Learning to Rank) predictor designed to identify the binding mode of peptide-MHC (pMHC) complexes.
It is trained to predict the most accurate ranking of an ensemble of pMHC conformations and outperforms classical peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous ML-based binding pose predictors.
RankMHC can be used with various pMHC structural modeling tools that employ different structural modeling protocols.
