RankMHC is a tool designed for predicting the binding affinity between peptides and MHC (Major Histocompatibility Complex) molecules.
It leverages machine learning techniques to rank peptides based on their likelihood of binding to specific MHC alleles.
This is crucial in understanding immune responses, as MHC molecules present peptide fragments on cell surfaces for T cell recognition.
RankMHC assists researchers in identifying potential T cell epitopes, which are key targets for vaccine development and immunotherapy.
By accurately predicting peptide-MHC interactions, RankMHC aids in narrowing down candidate peptides for experimental validation, thereby accelerating the process of identifying immunologically relevant targets.
