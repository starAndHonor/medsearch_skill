RankMHC is a Learning to Rank (LTR)-based predictor designed to identify the most accurate ranking of peptide-MHC (pMHC) structural models.
It excels in predicting the optimal binding mode of pMHC complexes by outperforming traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous machine learning-based binding pose predictors.
RankMHC's versatility lies in its compatibility with various pMHC structural modeling tools, regardless of their specific protocols, making it a robust and adaptable tool in the field of structural bioinformatics.
