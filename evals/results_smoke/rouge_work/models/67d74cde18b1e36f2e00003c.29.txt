RankMHC is an LTR-based pMHC binding mode identification predictor that is specifically trained to predict the most accurate ranking of an ensemble of pMHC conformations.
It is designed to identify the binding modes of peptide-MHC (pMHC) complexes, which are crucial for immune recognition and T-cell activation.
According to recent studies, Class I Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) molecules play a central role in immunology by binding to intracellular peptides and displaying them at the cell surface for recognition by T-cells.
RankMHC aims to predict the correct ranking of pMHC conformations, which is essential for understanding the interactions between T-cells and pMHC complexes, and has the potential to facilitate the design of novel therapeutic strategies targeting these complexes.
By accurately predicting the binding modes of pMHC complexes, RankMHC can contribute to a better understanding of immune recognition and the development of new treatments for diseases.
