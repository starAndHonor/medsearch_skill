RankMHC is a Learning to Rank (LTR)-based predictor designed to identify peptide-MHC (pMHC) binding modes.
It is specifically trained to accurately rank ensembles of pMHC conformations, determining which structural models are most likely to represent the native binding mode.
RankMHC demonstrates superior performance compared to traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous machine learning-based binding pose predictors.
A key advantage of RankMHC is its versatility, as it can be integrated with various pMHC structural modeling tools that employ different protocols, making it a valuable tool for immunological research and potentially for applications in vaccine design and immunotherapy.
