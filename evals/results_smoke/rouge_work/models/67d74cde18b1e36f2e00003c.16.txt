RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR) based predictor designed to identify the most accurate peptide binding pose from an ensemble of peptide-Major Histocompatibility Complex (pMHC) conformations.
It excels over traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous ML-based predictors.
Compatible with various pMHC structural modeling tools and protocols, RankMHC aims to enhance peptide antigen identification.
This improvement is crucial for developing effective therapies against diseases like bacterial and viral infections, as well as cancer, by providing a more accurate pMHC structural analysis.
