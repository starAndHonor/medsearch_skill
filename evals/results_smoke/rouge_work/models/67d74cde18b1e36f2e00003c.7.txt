RankMHC is a computational tool designed to predict peptide-MHC binding modes and rank potential binding peptides to MHC molecules.
It uses a Learning-to-Rank approach to identify the most accurate peptide-MHC binding mode from structural models, outperforming traditional scoring functions and previous machine learning predictors.
Additionally, RankMHC evaluates interaction energies using statistical pairwise contact potentials, effectively identifying peptides with high binding potential without relying solely on allele-specific binding motifs.
