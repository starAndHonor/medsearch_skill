RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR)-based predictor designed for identifying binding modes of peptide-MHC (pMHC) complexes.
It is specifically trained to accurately rank an ensemble of pMHC conformations, outperforming traditional peptide-ligand scoring functions and previous machine learning-based binding pose predictors.
RankMHC can be integrated with various pMHC structural modeling tools that utilize different protocols, enhancing its versatility in predicting the most representative peptide poses within the MHC-I cleft.
Additionally, it calculates MHC-I/II binding affinities across the human leukocyte antigen landscape, contributing to the understanding of immune responses and aiding in drug and vaccine design.
