RankMHC is a computational tool used for predicting and ranking peptide-MHC (Major Histocompatibility Complex) binding affinities.
It is designed to help identify which peptides are most likely to bind to MHC molecules, which is a crucial step in understanding immune responses and developing vaccines or immunotherapies.
RankMHC uses algorithms to analyze peptide sequences and predict their binding strength to specific MHC alleles, aiding researchers in prioritizing peptides for further experimental validation.
