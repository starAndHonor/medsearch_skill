RankMHC is a computational tool that utilizes machine learning to evaluate and rank structural models of class I peptide MHC complexes.
Its primary goal is to determine which model best represents the interaction between peptides and MHC molecules, enhancing our understanding of immune responses.
By assessing these models, RankMHC contributes to fields like vaccine development and drug target identification, aiding in the accurate prediction of antigen presentation and T cell recognition.
