RankMHC is a computational tool designed to predict the binding affinity of peptides to Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) molecules.
This prediction is important for understanding immune responses, as MHC molecules present peptide fragments to T cells, which then recognize and respond to these antigens.
RankMHC employs machine learning algorithms and extensive datasets of known peptide-MHC interactions to rank peptides based on their likelihood of binding to specific MHC alleles.
This tool is valuable in immunology research, vaccine development, and cancer immunotherapy, where identifying potential epitopes is essential.
