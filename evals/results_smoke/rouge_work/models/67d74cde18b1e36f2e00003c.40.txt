RankMHC is a Learning-to-Rank (LTR) based predictor designed to identify the most accurate peptide binding pose in class-I Peptide-Major Histocompatibility Complex (pMHC) structural models.
It ranks an ensemble of pMHC conformations, outperforming traditional scoring functions and previous Machine Learning-based predictors.
Notably, RankMHC is versatile and compatible with various pMHC structural modeling tools and protocols, enhancing its utility in predicting optimal pMHC conformations.
This capability can be leveraged to improve the identification of peptide antigens, potentially aiding in the development of therapies for diseases such as bacterial and viral infections, and cancer.
