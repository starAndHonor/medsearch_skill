RankMHC is a computational tool used in the field of immunology to predict the binding affinity of peptides to major histocompatibility complex (MHC) molecules.
It is often used in the context of mass spectrometry data to help identify potential epitopes for vaccine development or disease research.
RankMHC uses algorithms to rank peptides based on their likelihood of binding to specific MHC alleles, aiding in the identification of peptides that may elicit an immune response.
